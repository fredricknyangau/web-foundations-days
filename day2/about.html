About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app to help you capture and organize your ideas. By keeping important thoughts and tasks in one place, it helps you stay focused and productive.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page and enter a note in the text field.
  2. Select Add Note to add it to your list.
  3. Review your notes whenever you need a reminder.

Features

  • Capture ideas in a simple note list.
  • See how many notes you have.
  • Use the Home and About pages to navigate the app.
